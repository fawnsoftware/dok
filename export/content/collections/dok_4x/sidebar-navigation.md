# Sidebar

:::lead
Dok ships with multiple sidebar navigation templates to choose from.
:::

```files
└── docs/partials/
    ├── _sidebar_nav_collapse.antlers.html
    └── _sidebar_nav_default.antlers.html
```


The `default` partial is used as the starting template. Feel free to try the others and see what works best. Just update the references to it in your views.
